  • Speedspeed = distance ÷ timev = s / t
  • Accelerationacceleration = change in velocity ÷ timea = Δv / t
  • Forceforce = mass × accelerationF = ma
  • Momentummomentum = mass × velocityp = mv
  • Pressurepressure = force ÷ areap = F / A
  • Densitydensity = mass ÷ volumeρ = m / V
  • Work donework = force × distanceW = Fs
  • Powerpower = work ÷ timeP = W / t

  • GPEΔGPE = mass × g × ΔheightΔEₚ = mgΔh

  • Voltage & resistancevoltage = current × resistanceV = IR
  • Electrical powerpower = voltage × currentP = IV

How you are marked

0 → 8

Each criterion is marked out of 8. This is what an answer in the 7–8 band does.

A

Knowing and understanding

Explain the science, apply it to unfamiliar problems, and analyse information to make a judgement.

Criterion A · /8
B

Inquiring and designing

A testable hypothesis with scientific reasoning, and a complete, safe, logical method.

Criterion B · /8
C

Processing and evaluating

Present and transform data correctly, interpret it, then evaluate the method and suggest improvements.

Criterion C · /8
D

Reflecting on impacts

Explain how science solves a real problem, weigh its implications, and cite your sources.

Criterion D · /8
